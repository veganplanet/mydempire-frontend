What is Diwali?

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is one of South Asia’s most cherished festivals. Traditionally a Hindu festival, it is also observed by Jain, Sikh, and some Buddhist communities, and is celebrated by people across the globe.

Festival of light & prosperity

🌟 Light over darkness

Diwali represents light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance, and good over evil. It is a time for gratitude, hope, family, friendship, and fresh beginnings.

🏛️ Why it fits MydEmpire

MydEmpire is built around production, trade, cooperation, and prosperity. A shared festival gives every empire a simple way to contribute goods, celebrate achievement, and welcome a brighter season together.
