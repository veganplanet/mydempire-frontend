Genesis Rulebook

Core Gameplay Loop

Pack → Land + Blueprint + EMP → Build Factory → Generate EP → Maintain and Upgrade → Earn HIVE Share

MydEmpire rewards productive empires. Land and blueprints become useful when turned into active factories. Players can also earn through Goods, activities, events, trading, and the Imperial Mint.

Genesis Strategic Assets

  • WRIT: 1 for every 10 packs opened in the same order; tradable; holding one gives 5% faster required active days.
  • Relic: 0.25% drop chance; +3% EP each; maximum 2 count for a 6% boost.
  • Founder boost: cumulative EP milestones apply at 50, 100, 150, 300, and 500 opened Genesis packs.

The rule applies whether a higher-tier factory was built directly or upgraded later. A blocked factory displays “Max tier reached”.

Blueprints

Blueprints define factory industry and starting tier. Construction consumes the Blueprint. Industries are Food, Textile, Pharma, Chemical, and Supermarket.

Extra Blueprint Minting

  • Only for eligible unused slots on L2 and L3 Land
  • Cost: 50% of current pack price, paid in EMP
  • Maximum cost: 250 EMP
  • Uses the standard B1–B5 probability table

Empire Production (EP)

EP is current production power, not a stored token. Active factories generate EP from Land base, Blueprint tier, factory level, and eligible boosts.

Component Values
Land base L1 10 · L2 20 · L3 35
Blueprint multiplier B1 1.0 · B2 1.2 · B3 1.5 · B4 1.9 · B5 2.5
Factory level multiplier Level 1–5: 1.0 · 1.25 · 1.5 · 1.75 · 2.0

Maintenance

Each standard factory operates on a 7-day maintenance cycle.

  • B1 and B2 → 1 EMP
  • B3 and B4 → 2 EMP
  • B5 → 3 EMP
  • Renewal opens in the final 2 days
  • An expired factory stops producing until restored

Resources and Premium Materials

EMP

Internal utility for construction, upgrades, maintenance, crates, minting, trading, and other game actions. Pack EMP is soulbound.

Imperial Fragments

Rare tradable material from selected rewards, used in premium Foundry recipes.

SMP

Special crafting material earned through activities and consumed in premium recipes.

EMP no longer has a fixed shop price. Its HIVE value is discovered through the Imperial Exchange liquidity pool.

Daily HIVE Rewards

MydEmpire does not print a reward token. HIVE rewards come from the treasury and are shared according to each empire’s EP at the daily 00:00 UTC snapshot.

Player Reward = (Player EP ÷ Global EP) × Daily Reward Pool
  • Unclaimed rewards remain available for up to 7 days
  • Rewards outside the claim window lapse and return to the treasury
  • Only eligible productive EP participates

You are not earning tokens. You are earning a share.

B3–B5 Daily EMP Support

Eligible active B3, B4, and B5 factories can receive daily EMP support. It is credited automatically, recorded in daily history, and shown in notifications. Current rates and multipliers appear in the live game controls.

Goods Production and Redemption

Goods are a separate collectible economy layer. They do not create EP or directly increase HIVE rewards.

Claim Capacity

  • Below 100 EP/day → not eligible
  • 100–499 → 30% of factories
  • 500–999 → 75%
  • 1,000+ → 100%

Claims rotate factories and use a 24-hour timer.

Goods Value

  • B1–B5 produce 1–5 products
  • R1–R5: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 PV
  • Quality: 1★ ×1, 2★ ×2, 3★ ×3
  • EMP redemption depends on pool size and total redeemed PV

Goods and EMP may be sent to another verified Hive user for a 1 EMP fee. Valuable 3★, R4, and R5 Goods receive extra selection protection.

Daily Activity and Imperial Wheel

Normal empire actions award Activity Points (AP). Every 50 AP earns one wheel spin. Rewards include EMP, SMP, and Imperial Fragments.

Imperial Supply Crates

  • First daily crate is free for eligible producers; otherwise 25 EMP
  • Second through fourth crates cost 15 EMP each
  • 3-hour cooldown between openings
  • Possible EMP and Imperial Fragment rewards

Imperial Mint

The Imperial Mint is a special EMP-producing industry. It produces no EP, HIVE, Goods, or AP.

Construction

  • 1 Mint Blueprint
  • 1 Construction Ticket
  • 1 unused L1 Land
  • Instant construction

Production

  • Base: 10 EMP/day
  • Claim stored EMP any time
  • Maintenance: 10 EMP/week

Long-term Boost

  • +5% every 100 active days
  • Non-compounding
  • Maximum 20 EMP/day at 1,000 days

Mint Blueprints are event/reward assets and do not come from normal packs or standard Blueprint minting.

Marketplace, Exchange, and Foundry

Marketplace

List supported assets such as Blueprints, Packs, Land, premium materials, and Construction Tickets. Currency and fees appear before confirmation.

Imperial Exchange

Buy or sell EMP against the HIVE/EMP liquidity pool. Swaps use a 0.5% fee and quotes expire after 15 minutes.

Imperial Foundry

Combine premium materials and EMP in fixed recipes to create selected assets, including high-tier random Blueprints.

The Exchange rate changes with pool activity. Review the displayed quote, fee, and expected output before confirming.

Operations, Fulfillment, and Events

The Empire Hub also contains Empire Operations, Factory Fulfillment, management actions, Foundry crafting, and time-limited events such as the Imperial Trade Fair. Requirements, cooldowns, deductions, and prizes appear in live panels because event rules may change.

New Player Guide

  1. Connect Hive Keychain and open a pack
  2. Review Land, Blueprint, and EMP
  3. Build within Land zoning rules
  4. Maintain factories and monitor EP
  5. Claim HIVE before the claim window expires
  6. Use Goods, activities, crates, events, trading, and upgrades to expand

When a live panel displays a current rate, timer, seasonal rule, or confirmation amount, that live value takes priority over this general rulebook.
