🏭 IIB: Info & How to Play

The Industrial Investment Board lets you allocate Industrial Investment Credits (IIC) to five industries. Their values move during the week; a positive weekly result in an individual industry can earn EMP.

Who can participate?

Players need at least 1,000 EP/day to invest, withdraw, or buy IIC. Sign in with Hive Keychain. Eligible players receive 1,000 free IIC each week; the board checks eligibility and allocates it automatically. If your account is not yet ready, use Join this week.

Free weekly investment

Starting 30 November, choose an industry and invest your 1,000 free IIC once. This investment stays locked until the weekly reset. It earns EMP if profitable and expires whether profitable or not; eligible players receive a fresh 1,000 free IIC for the new week. Purchased IIC is held separately and can be invested or withdrawn during the week.

How to invest purchased IIC

  1. Choose Food, Textile, Pharma, Chemical, or Supermarket.
  2. Enter the amount of available IIC you want to invest.
  3. Select Invest IIC. Your order executes immediately using the latest published industry value.

You can split your available IIC across industries. Your investment value may go up or down; returns are not guaranteed.

What makes the market move?

At each two-hour update, the market combines three MydEmpire activity signals with a random movement. Each industry is calculated independently. The final movement is limited to −2.5% to +2.5% per update; changes compound over the week.

  • Active factories: the number of active factories in that industry compared with earlier recorded snapshots. More than the recent average adds upward pressure; fewer adds downward pressure.
  • Goods claimed: the number of goods claimed from that industry over the last 24 hours compared with the average of the previous seven 24-hour periods. More adds upward pressure; fewer adds downward pressure.
  • Goods redeemed: the PV of that industry’s goods redeemed in the last 24 hours compared with the previous seven 24-hour periods. More redeemed PV adds upward pressure; less adds downward pressure.
  • Random movement: an independent random adjustment can push the industry up or down at each update.

Each game-data factor can contribute up to ±0.30 percentage points per update. The random component ranges from −1.60 to +1.60 percentage points. The factors and random component combine for the update; the chart shows the actual recorded result and its components. If there is no usable comparison history, the affected factor contributes zero until data becomes available.

To limit one player’s influence, each account contributes at most 10 active factories per industry, 100 claimed goods per industry per 24-hour period, and 20,000 redeemed PV per industry per 24-hour period. Test accounts are excluded from these inputs.

IIC investments and withdrawals do not change the market values. The market does not use real-world prices, operating costs, or news and events. There is no guaranteed gain or winning industry.

Industry movements and the chart

Industry values are updated approximately every two hours. The percentage on an industry card shows its movement since the start of the current week. Select an industry above the chart to see its recorded updates. A green chart means its latest weekly change is zero or positive; red means it is negative.

Example: If you invest 1,000 IIC at the start of the week and that industry rises by 20%, the investment is worth approximately 1,200 IIC. If it falls by 10%, it is worth approximately 900 IIC. Your personal return depends on when you invested and any withdrawals.

Withdraw or switch industries

Select an industry, enter an amount up to your investment’s current IIC value, and choose Withdraw IIC. The withdrawal executes at the latest published value. A 0.25% fee is deducted from the amount withdrawn. The remaining IIC becomes available to invest again, including in another industry.

Example: Withdrawing 1,000 IIC incurs a 2.5 IIC fee and returns 997.5 IIC to your available balance.

Buy more IIC with HIVE

When purchases are enabled, 1 HIVE buys 1,000 IIC. Enter a positive whole number of HIVE and approve the payment in Hive Keychain. The payment goes to @mydempiregain. IIC is credited after the Hive transfer is verified and irreversible, which may take time.

If the payment is sent but credit is pending, keep the transaction ID and use Retry payment credit. Retrying checks the same payment; it does not send HIVE again. Do not send a second payment just because the first is pending.

Live profit or loss

Your account shows your overall weekly profit or loss and a separate result for each industry you traded. Each industry’s result includes its withdrawals after fees, plus the latest value of its remaining investment, minus the IIC invested in it. The estimated EMP reward adds the positive results from each industry. A loss in one industry does not cancel a profit in another. Values are estimates until the weekly reset.

Weekly leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks the top 100 participating players by estimated EMP reward this week, and shows the previous settled week's results. Overall return is the account's total IIC profit or loss divided by its free, purchased and carried-forward IIC for the week. Buying IIC during the week changes that denominator. Test accounts are excluded. A higher investment may earn a larger EMP reward.

Weekly rewards and reset

Each week runs from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. At the end, the board calculates each industry’s result separately, including withdrawals after fees and the closing value of remaining investments, less the amount invested in that industry. Each positive industry result earns 1 IIC profit = 1 EMP; industries with zero or negative results earn no EMP. A loss in one industry does not reduce the reward from another.

Example: If Food earns 100 IIC and Pharma loses 40 IIC, Food earns 100 EMP and Pharma earns no EMP. Your combined account result is +60 IIC.

Players who make at least one free investment, purchased investment or withdrawal trade during the week receive 3 AP at settlement. Simply receiving the free allocation does not count as a trade.

What carries forward?

The free weekly allocation expires at the reset. Eligible players receive a new 1,000 free IIC for the new week. Purchased IIC, including purchased IIC carried in from earlier weeks, carries forward as available IIC after losses in purchased investments. Profits paid as EMP do not offset losses in other industries. Positive net profit is paid as EMP rather than added to the purchased IIC carried forward. Investments close at the weekly reset, so choose industries again in the new week.

Example: If 1,000 purchased IIC in one industry falls to 900, 900 purchased IIC remains. The separate free allocation expires and an eligible player receives a fresh 1,000 free IIC next week.

The board is in testing. Check your balance after settlement and keep payment transaction IDs until your IIC has been credited.
