🏭 IIB: Info & How to Play

The Industrial Investment Board lets you allocate Industrial Investment Credits (IIC) to five industries. Their values move during the week; a positive overall result can earn EMP.

Who can participate?

Players need at least 1,000 EP/day to invest, withdraw, or buy IIC. Sign in with Hive Keychain. Eligible players receive 1,000 free IIC each week; the board checks eligibility and allocates it automatically. If your account is not yet ready, use Join this week.

How to invest

  1. Choose Food, Textile, Pharma, Chemical, or Supermarket.
  2. Enter the amount of available IIC you want to invest.
  3. Select Invest IIC. Your order executes immediately using the latest published industry value.

You can split your available IIC across industries. Your investment value may go up or down; returns are not guaranteed.

Industry movements and the chart

Industry values are updated approximately every two hours. The percentage on an industry card shows its movement since the start of the current week. Select an industry above the chart to see its recorded updates. A green chart means its latest weekly change is zero or positive; red means it is negative.

Example: If you invest 1,000 IIC at the start of the week and that industry rises by 20%, the investment is worth approximately 1,200 IIC. If it falls by 10%, it is worth approximately 900 IIC. Your personal return depends on when you invested and any withdrawals.

Withdraw or switch industries

Select an industry, enter an amount up to your investment’s current IIC value, and choose Withdraw IIC. The withdrawal executes at the latest published value. A 0.25% fee is deducted from the amount withdrawn. The remaining IIC becomes available to invest again, including in another industry.

Example: Withdrawing 1,000 IIC incurs a 2.5 IIC fee and returns 997.5 IIC to your available balance.

Buy more IIC with HIVE

When purchases are enabled, 1 HIVE buys 1,000 IIC. Enter a positive whole number of HIVE and approve the payment in Hive Keychain. The payment goes to @mydempiregain. IIC is credited after the Hive transfer is verified and irreversible, which may take time.

If the payment is sent but credit is pending, keep the transaction ID and use Retry payment credit. Retrying checks the same payment; it does not send HIVE again. Do not send a second payment just because the first is pending.

Weekly rewards and reset

Each week runs from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. At the end, the board adds your available IIC and the closing value of your investments, then subtracts your free allocation, purchased IIC, and purchased IIC carried in from the previous week. Positive net profit is rewarded at 1 IIC profit = 1 EMP; zero or negative net profit receives no EMP reward. Withdrawal fees affect the result.

Players who make at least one investment or withdrawal trade during the week receive 3 AP at settlement. Simply receiving the free allocation does not count as a trade.

What carries forward?

The free weekly allocation expires at the reset. Eligible players receive a new 1,000 free IIC for the new week. Purchased IIC, including purchased IIC carried in from earlier weeks, carries forward as available IIC; it is reduced proportionally if the overall account ends the week at a loss. Positive net profit is paid as EMP rather than added to the purchased IIC carried forward. Investments close at the weekly reset, so choose industries again in the new week.

Loss example: With 1,000 free IIC and 1,000 purchased IIC, if your total ends at 1,800 IIC, the 200 IIC loss is shared proportionally. About 900 purchased IIC carries forward; the free portion expires. An eligible player then receives the new 1,000 free IIC.

The board is in testing. Check your balance after settlement and keep payment transaction IDs until your IIC has been credited.
