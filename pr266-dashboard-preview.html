📯 AMBASSADOR CHALLENGE

Heralds of the Empire

Share your genuine MydEmpire experience and invite new Hive players to discover the game.

💰 EMP Reward Rules

If the challenge receives up to 5 valid posts, each approved participant will receive 500 EMP.

If the challenge receives 6 or more valid posts, the full 5,000 EMP pool will be divided equally among all approved participants.

How to qualify

  1. Publish one original Hive post of at least 300 words.
  2. Include at least one screenshot from your MydEmpire gameplay.
  3. Share your genuine experience and explain why other Hive gamers should try MydEmpire.
  4. Include the official MydEmpire website and Discord links.
  5. Tag and personally invite 5 genuine Hive friends who are not already MydEmpire players.
  6. Submit your post link in the designated MydEmpire Discord channel.

Fair-play rules

  • Only one rewarded post per player and Hive account.
  • Copied, duplicate, low-effort, mass-tagging or alternate-account entries will not qualify.
  • Every submitted post will be reviewed manually.
  • EMP rewards will be distributed after the current round ends.
  • Each round remains open for its current half of the month.
🎟 Imperial Mint Blueprint Lucky Draw

If at least 6 valid posts are approved, all approved participants will enter a public lucky draw.

One winner will receive a tradable Imperial Mint Blueprint.
